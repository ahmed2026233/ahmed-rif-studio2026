The challenge

A restaurant website should make choosing dinner feel easy.

For a restaurant, visitors usually want answers quickly: What do you serve? Does the place fit the occasion? Where are you? Can I reserve a table?

The concept was built around those decisions rather than around decoration alone. The interface gives the menu, atmosphere, reservation and location clear roles in the customer journey.

Project snapshot

A restaurant experience with a clear job.

A focused case study showing how visual design, UX and interaction can support a hospitality business.
